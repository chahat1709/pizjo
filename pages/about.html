Our Story

Born out of late-night cravings and a serious love for cheese.

How It Started

From a Simple Idea to Ahmedabad's Cheesiest Obsession

Every great pizza starts with a story — ours started with a craving at 2 AM and the realization that Amraiwadi deserved better pizza. Not the mass-produced, frozen-dough kind. The real thing.

We set out to build a place where every pizza is handcrafted with fresh dough (rested for 24 hours), loaded with 100% Amul Mozzarella, and baked to perfection. No shortcuts. No compromises. Just pure, oven-baked happiness.

Today, Pizjo serves 155+ items across 15 categories — from our signature Bahubali Pizza to crispy momos, cheesy calizzas, stuffed garlic breads, and refreshing coolers. Every item on our menu is 100% pure vegetarian.

Our Values

What Makes Us Different

🧀

100% Amul Mozzarella

We only use genuine Amul Mozzarella cheese. Real cheese, real stretch, real taste. No substitutes. Ever.

🫓

24-Hour Rested Dough

Our dough is made fresh daily and rested for 24 hours. This slow fermentation creates the perfect crust — light, airy, and full of flavour.

💚

Pure Vegetarian

Our entire kitchen is 100% pure vegetarian. Every ingredient, every sauce, every topping is carefully sourced and verified.

🔥

No Shortcuts

We don't do frozen dough. We don't do processed ingredients. Every item is handcrafted, oven-baked, and served with love.
