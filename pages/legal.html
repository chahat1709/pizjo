Privacy Policy

1. Data Collection

Pizjo Pizza collects the following data to provide and improve our services:

  • Cart items (product names, prices, quantities)
  • Performance metrics (FPS data for website optimization)
  • Contact details (name, phone, email, and message) are placed into a WhatsApp message only when you choose to submit the form

2. How We Use Your Data

Collected data is used to:

  • Process your pizza orders via WhatsApp
  • Improve website performance and user experience
  • Understand customer preferences and menu popularity
  • Communicate with you about your orders and promotions
  • Provide customer support

3. Data Storage & Retention

Your data is stored in the following ways:

  • Cart data: Stored locally in your browser using localStorage (cleared when you clear browser data)
  • Contact form data: Sent to our business WhatsApp account only after you submit; Pizjo does not store it in this website
  • Optional telemetry: Available only on designated staging builds and sent only after explicit opt-in

Note: No personal data is stored on our servers. Cart data is browser-only and temporary.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We share your data with:

  • WhatsApp: Your order details are sent to our business WhatsApp account for order processing
  • Unsplash: Pizza images are loaded from Unsplash CDN
  • Google Fonts: Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts CDN

No data is sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Telemetry & Analytics

Analytics Status: Loading...

Analytics are currently in staging mode. When enabled, we collect:

  • FPS performance metrics
  • Page load times
  • Navigation patterns

You can enable or disable analytics at any time using the controls below.

Note: Analytics are staging-only and do not collect data in production.

6. Your Rights & Choices

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Opt-out of analytics: You can disable telemetry collection at any time
  • Delete your data: Clear your browser's localStorage to remove cart data
  • No tracking cookies: We do not use tracking cookies
  • No mandatory data collection: You can browse our menu without storing cart data

7. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe we have collected information about a child under 13, please contact us immediately.

8. Security Measures

We implement the following security measures:

  • XSS Prevention: All user-generated content is escaped before rendering
  • Rate Limiting: Prevents abuse of our order system
  • Same-Origin Policy: Service worker only handles requests from our domain
  • Input Validation: All form inputs are validated before processing
  • HTTPS: All connections use HTTPS encryption

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. We will take reasonable steps to let you know when changes are made, such as sending an email notification or posting a notice on our website.
