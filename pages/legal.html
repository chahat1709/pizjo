Privacy & Terms

Plain English. No fine print. Just the honest version.

Last updated: November 2025

1. Privacy Policy

Pizjo runs this website (pizjo.in) so people can look at our menu, find our store, and order pizza. We collect the absolute minimum data needed to do that well. This page explains exactly what we collect, why, and what we will never do with it.

2. What we collect

  • Order enquiries (if you submit the contact form). Your name, phone number, optional email, and your message. We use this only to reply to you about your enquiry. We do not add you to marketing lists, and we do not sell or share your details with anyone.
  • Order contents from your cart. Stored only in your own browser (see cookies below). We do not transmit your cart contents to our servers until you hit "Order on WhatsApp" — at which point you are forwarded to WhatsApp to talk to us directly. We never see that as a server-side order, it lives entirely in your browser.
  • Server logs. Like every website, our hosting provider records your IP address, the page you visited, and the time. We use this only to debug problems and prevent abuse. Logs are kept for at most 90 days.

3. Cookies & local storage

We use one piece of browser storage: your cart contents. It stays in your browser's localStorage and is not a cookie. Nothing tracking, advertising, or analytics-based is set by this site. No Facebook pixel, no Google Analytics, no third-party trackers. Your cart stays on your device; if you clear your browser data it disappears.

4. Third parties

A few things on this site are loaded from elsewhere. Those services may collect their own data according to their own privacy policies:

  • WhatsApp (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd). When you click "Order on WhatsApp" you are redirected to whatsapp.com or wa.me, governed by WhatsApp's privacy policy. We only pre-fill the message; we do not send anything to WhatsApp directly.
  • Google Maps. Embedded on the About and Contact pages to show our shop location. Google may set cookies when you load those pages, governed by Google's privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts. We load web fonts from fonts.googleapis.com so the typography looks consistent across browsers. Google may log the request.

We do not embed social-login, ad networks, or analytics on this site. Pizjo's Instagram, Facebook, and Zomato pages are linked but not embedded — clicking those links sends you to the respective platform.
