# Pizjo Brand Guidelines v1.0

> Last updated: 2026-07-31
> Status: Official

## 1. Brand Persona & Voice

**The Persona: The Unapologetic Rebel**
Pizjo is the anti-diet. We don't pretend to be a health food brand. We are the masters of comfort food, focusing on extreme indulgence without compromising on ingredient quality. We are bold, loud, and confident in our craft.

**The Tone:**
- **Rebellious but Welcoming:** We playfully mock diet culture but take our food seriously.
- **Punchy & Direct:** Short, impactful sentences. No corporate fluff.
- **Confident:** We know we make the best pizza in Amraiwadi, and we aren't afraid to say it.

**Tagline:**
> *"The People Your Diet Warned You About"*

---

## 2. Messaging Pillars

Our messaging revolves around two core pillars that set us apart from cheap fast food:

### Pillar 1: Uncompromising Quality (The "No Shortcuts" Rule)
We fight the perception that affordable pizza uses fake cheese. 
- **Keywords:** 100% Amul Mozzarella, 24-hour rested dough, baked-to-order, handcrafted, real cheese.
- **Usage:** Always remind the customer *why* our food tastes better. "Fresh Dough. Real Cheese. No Shortcuts."

### Pillar 2: Accessible Indulgence
We offer premium quality at neighborhood prices.
- **Keywords:** Hot, fast, Amraiwadi's favorite, comfort.
- **Usage:** Position Pizjo as the go-to spot for everyday cravings, not just special occasions.

## 3. Visual Identity (Grunge / Neon)

Our visual aesthetic is loud and underground. We use sharp corners, high-contrast colors, and expressive typography. 

### Color Palette

| Name | Hex | Usage |
|------|-----|-------|
| **Amber Neon (Primary)** | `#FFBF00` | Backgrounds, primary buttons, accents. The core of our neon vibe. |
| **Umber Raw (Dark)** | `#3E2723` | Text, heavy borders, dark backgrounds. Provides the gritty contrast to the neon. |
| **Off-White (Surface)** | `#F5F2EB` | Main page backgrounds and cards. |
| **Veg Green (Semantic)** | `#0F8A65` | Used specifically for vegetarian indicators and positive actions. |

### Typography

Our typography mixes clean legibility with chaotic, hand-drawn elements to reinforce the rebel persona.

| Role | Font Family | Usage |
|------|-------------|-------|
| **Display / Headlines** | `Bricolage Grotesque` | Main section titles, bold statements. Always uppercase. |
| **Body Text** | `Hanken Grotesk` | Paragraphs, descriptions, readable UI text. |
| **Marker (Accent)** | `Permanent Marker` | Prices, massive numbers, "loud" badges. |
| **Scribble (Accent)** | `Caveat` | Playful annotations, secondary accents, footer text. |

### Structural Elements

- **Borders & Shadows:** We use thick, solid borders (`4px solid var(--umber-raw)`) and hard, non-blurred drop shadows (e.g., `4px 4px 0 var(--umber-raw)`) to create a "sticker" or "brutalist" effect.
- **Corners:** Absolutely no rounded corners (`border-radius: 0`). Grunge is sharp and angular.
- **Motion:** Interactions are snappy (`150ms`). Clickable elements physically depress (`scale(0.98)`) to feel tactile and responsive.
